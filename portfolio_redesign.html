About

7+ years shipping games that perform

I'm a Senior Unity Developer building games and XR products for mobile, console and immersive platforms. I specialize in modular game architecture and runtime performance — taking systems from unplayable frame rates to a stable 60 FPS by reworking CPU/GPU data flow and cutting garbage-collection pressure.

I've shipped Unity products for Samsung, mentored teams of developers, and spent 3+ years teaching Unity, game design and Scrum as a Game Development Instructor. Outside client work I'm a lifelong Star Wars fan and a die-hard tabletop RPG player — dice betrayals included.
